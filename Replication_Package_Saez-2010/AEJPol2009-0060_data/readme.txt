I. Data files situation:

The data used for this paper are public use tax return data that were purchased by NBER. Because those data
are licensed solely for NBER researchers, the NBER does not allow us to distribute them outside of NBER. 
Researchers wanting to do a replication would have to get access through NBER or purchase the data directly 
from the Internal Revenue Service.

II. Data Programs:

Three stata do files are included
1) eitc_main.do constructs the EITC working files, the EITC graphs, and the EITC elasticity table
2) bunchtaxinc.do constructs the tax working files, the tax graphs, and the tax elasticity tables 
3) eitc_elasticity_sub.do is a subroutine that is used by the other two programs to estimate elasticity 
estimates and standard errors
